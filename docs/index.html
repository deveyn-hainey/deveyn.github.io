About Me

I'm an engineer and data + product enthusiast based in Denver, Colorado, and I care deeply about building things that actually help people.

I graduated from the University of Colorado Denver with a BS in Computer Science, a minor in Data Science, and a certificate in Cybersecurity. Most recently, I've been at Quizlet, growing from a data science research assistant to an ML Associate to an AI & ML Engineer, working on the systems that power how millions of students study every day.

My work lives at the intersection of product and engineering. I dig into data to find what's actually hurting users, then build solutions to fix it. That might mean improving an AI grading model, redesigning how search results are ranked, or building tools that make complex data accessible to teams across the organization.

Outside of work, I'm happiest on a hiking trail somewhere in Colorado. You'll also find me in the kitchen experimenting with new recipes (the chocolate chip cookie is still a work in progress) or planning the next trip somewhere in the world.
