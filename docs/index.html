About Me

I'm a Machine Learning Engineer based in Denver, Colorado — passionate about building systems that scale and about getting ML to actually work in production.

I graduated from the University of Colorado Denver with a BS in Computer Science, a minor in Data Science, and a certificate in Cybersecurity. As an ML Engineer at Quizlet, I owned production ML systems end-to-end: selecting H100 GPUs for a grading service handling 100 RPS, designing LLM evaluation frameworks, and analyzing hundreds of millions of monthly user interactions.

I specialize in the full ML engineering stack — Python services (FastAPI/Gunicorn), vLLM/AWQ GPU serving, Kubernetes/GKE deployments, and Datadog observability. I love combining quantitative rigor (Cohen's κ, confidence intervals, Poisson load modeling) with real engineering to ship ML that actually works.

Outside of work, Colorado keeps me busy. I'm an avid hiker — there's something fitting about a data scientist who loves exploring unknown terrain. You'll also find me experimenting in the kitchen or planning the next adventure somewhere new in the world.
